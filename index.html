Plantar Fasciitis Treatment in Lighthouse Point

Plantar fasciitis causes sharp heel pain, especially when you first step out of bed or stand after sitting. The plantar fascia—a band of tissue running along the bottom of your foot—becomes inflamed, and the ache can make everyday walking miserable. Many people assume they'll need injections or surgery to find relief, but that's not always the case.

At McNamara Chiropractic Center in Lighthouse Point, we address plantar fasciitis through a combination of targeted therapies designed to reduce inflammation and restore function. Our approach includes custom orthotics fitted using PostureCheck® 3D foot scanning technology—these are personalized inserts that support your arch and redistribute pressure away from the irritated fascia. We also use Class IV laser therapy and shockwave treatment to promote healing at the source of the pain.

Spinal alignment matters more than you might think. When your pelvis, hips, or lower back are out of balance, they can alter how your feet strike the ground and increase stress on the plantar fascia. Dr. McNamara-Krauss will assess your full structure—not just your foot—to identify whether postural or structural issues are fueling your pain.

What We Address
